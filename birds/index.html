Old plates meet new data

Scientific names join the live archive to openly licensed natural-history art, with source and credit intact.

The plates come from open collections including Wikimedia Commons, the Biodiversity Heritage Library, Naturalis, and the National Gallery of Art. One modern open illustration stands in for the Ashy Prinia: its 1798–1805 British Library watercolor is catalogued, but not digitized.
