01 / how it works / no magic tricks

Bring the old paperwork.
Change what changed.

DeBabufy carries forward the stable facts, calculates the new values with explicit rules, and fills the portal in an ordinary Chrome session.

  1. 01Add the previous document

    Your source stays local on your computer.

  2. 02Enter this time’s amount

    Review every inferred party, date, and tax value.

  3. 03Log in normally

    You handle credentials, OTP, and CAPTCHA yourself.

  4. 04Watch it fill the portal

    DeBabufy stops before submission or payment.

03 / the ground rules

Boring by design.

Local by default

No filing backend, telemetry, or document upload service.

Deterministic

No model improvising with PANs, dates, amounts, or portal actions.

Human controlled

Login, OTP, review, submission, and payment stay with you.

Open to inspection

The code, workflow rules, issues, and releases are public on GitHub.
